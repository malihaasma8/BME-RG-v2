Smart Personalized Biomedical Engineering Systems: From Benchtop Testing to Commercialization

Date: October 2026   •   Time: 9:00 AM

Venue: Qatar University, H10 Auditorium

This workshop scopes the spectrum of personalized biomedical systems from research question to marketable solutions. Successfully designed and prototyped systems from the BME Research Group will be showcased, and a detailed roadmap to commercialization will be presented.

View Tentative Program
  • 9:00 – 9:20 — State of the Art and Vision in Smart Personalized BME Systems (Prof Metin Sitti, President of Koç University, Turkey)
  • 9:20 – 10:00 — R&D Journey Presentations for 6 Selected BME Projects
  • 10:00 – 11:00 — Live Demonstration & Operational Testing (BME RNG Member Prototypes)
  • 11:00 – 12:00 — Coffee Break & Networking
  • 12:00 – 12:20 — Building Entrepreneurial Mindsets (Dr Mohammed Salman Khan, BME RNG)
  • 12:20 – 12:40 — QU IP/SIEED Office Talks (to be confirmed)
